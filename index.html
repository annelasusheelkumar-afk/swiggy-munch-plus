Swiggy Munch+

One Route. One Delivery. Unlimited Convenience. Order your favorite food along with nearby essentials like cool drinks, water bottles, snacks, and kirana items — delivered together through a smart hyperlocal delivery network.

Why Swiggy Munch+?

🍔 Food + Essentials

Customers can order meals along with drinks, snacks, groceries, and daily-use items in a single checkout experience.

⚡ Faster Convenience

No need for multiple delivery apps or repeated delivery charges. Everything arrives together in one optimized route.

🏪 Support Local Kirana

Nearby kirana stores become hyperlocal partners, increasing their business through Swiggy's ecosystem.

🚴 Smart Delivery Flow

Delivery partners collect food from restaurants and additional items from nearby stores while traveling to the customer destination.

How It Works

1️⃣

Order Food

Select your favorite meal from Swiggy Food.

2️⃣

Add Essentials

Add drinks, snacks, water bottles, and kirana products.

3️⃣

Nearby Store Sync

Partner kirana stores prepare the required add-on items.

4️⃣

Single Route Delivery

Delivery partner collects both orders and delivers together.

Core Benefits

💰 Save Delivery Costs

Reduce duplicate delivery fees by combining multiple needs into one order.

🎬 Better User Experience

Customers can continue enjoying movies, cricket matches, or family time without interruption.

🌍 Reduced Traffic

One delivery route helps reduce unnecessary rider movement and fuel usage.

📈 Increased Revenue

Higher order value benefits restaurants, local stores, delivery partners, and Swiggy.
